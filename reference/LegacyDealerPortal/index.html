📰 Portal Updates & Announcements
2024
12 Sep 2024
NEW Apex Series Pre-Order Now Open
The Apex City, Apex Trail, and Apex Pro are now available for pre-order. Download the Apex Series Pre-Order Form and Pre-Order Discount Policy from the Documents section above. For discount queries, contact your regional representative. Please allow 2 business days for quote confirmation.
22 Aug 2024
IMPORTANT New Authorised Dealer Agreement — Action Required by 31 Dec 2024
BestRun EV Legal has issued an updated Authorised Dealer Agreement (ADA-Rev7) reflecting new EU EV distribution regulations effective January 2025. All active dealers must sign and return the new agreement. Download from Documents section above.
15 Jun 2024
UPDATED Warranty Claim Form Updated to Rev4
The warranty claim process has been updated. Form WC-Rev3 is no longer accepted. Please download and use WC-Rev4 for all warranty submissions from 01 July 2024 onwards. Key change: dealers must now include PDI (Pre-Delivery Inspection) checklist reference number in all claims.
10 Jan 2024
UPDATED Dealer Commercial Handbook 2024 (Rev 5.2)
Updated handbook includes revised dealer margin schedule, updated payment terms (Net-30 / Net-60 options), and new quarterly volume bonus thresholds. Supersedes all previous versions. Download Dealer Commercial Handbook 2024 »
2023
03 Nov 2023
INFO Portal Downtime — Infrastructure Migration
The dealer portal was unavailable from 03–05 November 2023 due to server migration. All document downloads and order submissions have been restored. If you submitted an order during this window and have not received confirmation, please resubmit or contact support.
17 Jul 2023
UPDATED EU EV Compliance Documentation Package Added
In response to updated EU regulation (EU 2023/858), BestRun EV has added a compliance documentation set for dealers operating in Germany, France, Netherlands, and Nordics. Package includes CE declarations, battery disposal instructions, and type approval certificates. Available on request from compliance@bestrun-ev.example.
02 Mar 2023
UPDATED Order Form Rev 8.0 — Replaces Rev 7.x
The standard order form has been revised (Rev 8.0) to support the new 4-digit dealer code format. Orders submitted on Rev 7.x forms will be rejected from 01 April 2023. Download the new form from the Documents section.
2022
14 Dec 2022
UPDATED Dealer Commercial Handbook 2023 Preview Available
A preview of the 2023 Dealer Commercial Handbook is available for download. Key changes: updated minimum order quantities for eScooter category, new spare parts margin schedule. Final version will be published 10 January 2023.
18 May 2022
INFO EMEA Dealer Conference 2022 — Presentations Available
Presentations from the EMEA Dealer Conference (Berlin, April 2022) are now available for download. Includes the product roadmap overview, Q&A transcript, and updated training materials. Access via the Training Materials section (login required).
07 Feb 2022
UPDATED Return & Replacement Policy Updated (Rev 2.3)
The dealer return and replacement policy has been updated to reflect new logistics partner arrangements. Return window for defective units extended from 14 to 30 days. Updated RMA form available in Documents section.
2021
09 Nov 2021
UPDATED Q4 2021 Price List — EUR and GBP Updated
Revised dealer price list effective 01 December 2021. EUR prices unchanged; GBP prices adjusted +3.2% to reflect currency fluctuation. Dealer margin schedule remains unchanged. Download from Price Lists section.

INFO COVID-19 Supply Chain Notice — Delivery Lead Times Extended
Due to ongoing global supply chain disruptions, delivery lead times for all EV models are currently 14–18 weeks (previously 8–10 weeks). BestRun EV will update this notice as conditions improve. We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your continued partnership.
